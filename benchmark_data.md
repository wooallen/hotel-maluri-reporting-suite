# Kuala Lumpur 4-Star Hotel Industry Benchmarks & Hotel Maluri Financial Comparison (YTD 2026)

## 1. Key Operational & Financial Benchmarks: KL 4-Star Midscale Hotels vs. Hotel Maluri (01.01.2026 - 30.06.2026)

### Metric 1: Occupancy Rate
- KL 4-Star Benchmark Target: 68.5% (Range: 65.0% - 72.0%)
- Hotel Maluri Current Period: 53.62% (14,363 rooms sold out of 26,788 available rooms across 148 inventory rooms)
- Variance: -14.88 percentage points (-21.7% relative shortfall)

### Metric 2: Average Daily Rate (ADR)
- KL 4-Star Benchmark Target: RM 265.00 (Range: RM 240.00 - RM 310.00)
- Hotel Maluri Current Period: RM 161.68 (Rooms Revenue RM 2,322,249.45 / 14,363 rooms)
- Variance: -RM 103.32 (-39.0% pricing discount relative to 4-star peer group)

### Metric 3: Revenue Per Available Room (RevPAR)
- KL 4-Star Benchmark Target: RM 181.53 (Range: RM 160.00 - RM 220.00)
- Hotel Maluri Current Period: RM 86.69 (Rooms Revenue RM 2,322,249.45 / 26,788 room nights)
- Variance: -RM 94.84 (-52.2% RevPAR yield deficit)

### Metric 4: Payroll & Staff Cost Intensity (Payroll-to-Revenue Ratio)
- KL 4-Star Benchmark Target: 35.0% (Upper operational threshold: 38.0%)
- Hotel Maluri Current Period: 50.55% (Staff Cost RM 1,455,632.75 on Total Revenue RM 2,879,557.55)
- Variance: +15.55 percentage points OVER benchmark ceiling (37.5% excess labor cost intensity)

### Metric 5: Staffing Efficiency & Labor Density
- KL 4-Star Benchmark Target: 0.45 staff/room (For 148 rooms = 67 Full-Time Equivalent staff)
- Housekeeping Load Benchmark: 16 room cleans per attendant shift
- Utility Intensity Benchmark: 7.2% of total revenue (~28-32 kWh / occupied room night)

### Metric 6: Gross Operating Profit (GOP) Margin
- KL 4-Star Benchmark Target: +32.5% GOP Margin
- Hotel Maluri Current Period: -30.46% Net Margin (-RM 877,109.62 net loss on RM 2.88M revenue)
- Operational Deficit Drivers: Severe pricing discount (ADR RM 161 vs RM 265 benchmark) combined with critical labor inefficiency (Payroll 50.55% vs 35% target).
